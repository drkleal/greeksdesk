Connect your charts once

The browser connector lets GreeksDesk capture the Quant Data and OptionsDepth chart tabs you choose. Routine updates then require no screenshots or sharing picker.

One-time Chrome setup

  1. Download the chart connector, then extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Open your chart tabs in this same Chrome profile and sign in.
  5. Click the Chrome puzzle-piece button, then GreeksDesk Chart Connector. Select your chart tabs and click Connect selected charts.
  6. Reload GreeksDesk. Click Update & analyze.

The connector may display Chrome's debugging notice briefly during capture. Its browser permission is broad, but this implementation only captures selected supported chart pages. It does not read passwords, cookies or API keys, navigate tabs, or place trades.

During a session

Keep the selected charts open, signed in and showing the panels and date you want analyzed. A screenshot captures what the chart has rendered, not every panel hidden elsewhere on the website. The app labels capture time separately from the chart's market-data time.

For repeated capture and analysis, enable Analyze after each auto update, then turn Auto on. Keep GreeksDesk visible. Capturing charts itself makes no paid market-data API request; API data and AI analysis follow your app settings.

After restarting Chrome or closing selected tabs, select the chart tabs again. Disconnect them at any time in the extension popup. This connector is not installed or verified in your browser until you complete setup.
